Privacy

A simple approach to your information.

PeachAvo3D is a brand of Peach & Avo Ventures LLP. This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, accounts or contact forms.

What we receive

When you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, contact details, project description, images, files and measurements.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to understand your request, reply to you, prepare a quote and deliver an agreed project. We do not sell your personal information.

Project files

Your project material is used only to assess, design and produce your project. Please do not send confidential material unless you are comfortable doing so; ask us if you need to discuss confidentiality first.

Contact

For privacy questions, email peachavo3d@peachandavo.co.uk.
