About PeachAvo3D

A thoughtful route from concept to physical object.

PeachAvo3D is a UK-based 3D design and printing studio under Peach & Avo Ventures LLP. We make technology feel more useful, more approachable and occasionally a little more delightful.

What guides the work

Curiosity, clear communication and care for the object itself.

Some projects begin with a broken plastic part. Others begin with an entrepreneur trying to test a product idea, or with a decorative concept that deserves to leave the screen. The common thread is a desire to make something considered and tangible.

We combine practical digital design with a creative eye, helping people move from an uncertain starting point to a clear next step. Every request is reviewed individually so we can be honest about suitability, scope and the best way forward.

What to include in an enquiry

  1. A photo, sketch or description of the idea
  2. Measurements or reference objects where useful
  3. How the object will be used—or the feeling you want it to create
  4. Quantity, preferred timing and anything you already know about materials or finish
