What we do

From the useful to the wonderfully unnecessary.

We design and print physical objects for real tasks, early products and creative expression. Start from an existing file or bring us the roughest version of an idea.

⌁

Custom 3D design

We translate your reference, idea or practical requirement into a design that works visually and physically.

  • Parts designed from a sketch, photo or measurements
  • Digital modelling and refinement
  • Design-for-print guidance
◫

Functional parts & improvements

Useful solutions for spaces, possessions and day-to-day problems that off-the-shelf products do not quite solve.

  • Replacement or repair parts
  • Organisers, mounts, holders and brackets
  • Bespoke fit and practical iteration
◒

Prototypes & small runs

Give a product concept a physical first version before you commit to a larger production decision.

  • Form, fit and function prototypes
  • Presentation and test pieces
  • FDM one-offs and low-volume runs
✦

Creative objects & figures

3D design can be useful without being functional. We welcome ideas that are decorative, expressive and delightfully specific.

  • Original figures and display pieces
  • Sculptural homeware and decorative objects
  • Personal creative concepts and gifts
